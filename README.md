# SMR-power-ammonia-cogeneration
Investigating the efficiency and feasibility of SMR cogeneration of power and ammonia in Europe.
Analysis is based on a thermodynamic model developed in DWSIM, wholesale electricity and ammonia prices in Europe, 
economic figures of SMRs from Idaho National Laboratoty, and techno-economic analysis of the chemical system based on "Analysis, Synthesis, and Design of Chemical
Processes" by Turton et al.
Operating cost of the nuclear reactor system are based 2 SMR units using the figures in for the three scenarios: https://gain.inl.gov/content/uploads/4/2024/11/INL-RPT-23-72972-Literature-Review-of-Adv-Reactor-Cost-Estimates.pdf

Electricity prices were taken from EMBER and averaged over a 5 year period from 2020-25. 
Techno-economic analysis of the system is basen on Chemical Plant 

The secondary steam loop model are taken from the TANDEM project which is based on the European SMR (E-SMR). Source: http://tandemproject.eu/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/D2_3_Modelica_models_description_for_the_tandem_library_V1-1.pdf

The SOEC and ammonia synthesis reactor models were adopted from other sources.
